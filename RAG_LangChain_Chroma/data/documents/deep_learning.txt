Deep Learning

Deep learning is a specialized area of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Neural networks are composed of interconnected units commonly called neurons. Layers of neurons transform input data into increasingly useful representations.

Deep learning has achieved strong results in image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, recommendation systems, and generative AI.

Convolutional neural networks are commonly associated with computer vision tasks.

Transformer architectures are widely used for natural language processing and modern generative AI systems. Transformers use attention mechanisms to process relationships between elements in input sequences.

Training deep learning models can require significant amounts of data and computational resources. Model evaluation should consider both performance and the limitations of the training data.
