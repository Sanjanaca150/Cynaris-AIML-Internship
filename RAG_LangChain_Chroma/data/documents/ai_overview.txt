Artificial Intelligence Overview

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can perform tasks such as understanding language, recognizing images, making predictions, solving problems, and supporting decision making.

Machine learning is an important subfield of artificial intelligence. Instead of explicitly programming every rule, machine learning systems learn patterns from data.

AI applications are used in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, customer service, manufacturing, and many other areas.

Modern AI systems can use machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and generative AI techniques.

Responsible AI development should consider reliability, transparency, privacy, security, fairness, and human oversight.
