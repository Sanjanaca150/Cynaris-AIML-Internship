## OBSERVATION

Dense Retrieval performed well for semantic queries where the meaning
of the question matched the content of the documents.

It performed better than BM25 for questions related to cloud computing,
software design and software engineering.

However, some unrelated software engineering content was retrieved for
the artificial intelligence questions. This shows that dense retrieval
can sometimes identify broad semantic similarity instead of exact
topic relevance.
